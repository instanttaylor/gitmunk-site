What it does

Stop checking repos one by one.

Gitmunk watches the folders where you keep your clones and gives you one place to understand what changed, catch up safely, and keep the whole workspace tidy.

06 — Ask AI

Ask your repository, not just its history.Very beta

Ask in plain language. Gitmunk can search files, read relevant code and diffs, and connect them to recent commits. Requires a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

Ask AI
Where is the acorn cache invalidated?
It happens in AcornStore.swift after a successful sync. The recent caching commit ([a1c4f]) added the write path but kept invalidation tied to the existing refresh flow. Apple Intelligence · on-device or Private Cloud Compute

Private by design.

Gitmunk uses Apple Intelligence. Requests run on-device when possible and can use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute on supported Macs for larger, smarter answers. Gitmunk includes no analytics or telemetry.
